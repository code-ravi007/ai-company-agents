# Product research brief

## Method
1. Restate the question in one sentence. If the request is broad, pick the decision it should inform ("should we build X", "how should X work").
2. Find 3-6 relevant existing products or approaches. For each: what they do, for whom, price if public, what users praise and complain about (reviews, forums, issue trackers).
3. Look for facts the decision depends on: standards, platform limits, legal or privacy constraints, typical expectations.
4. Prefer primary sources. Note the date of anything that may change (prices, versions).
5. Stop when more searching would not change the recommendation.

## Template

```markdown
# Research: <question>

**Recommendation:** <one or two sentences>

## What we need to decide
<the decision this informs>

## Existing solutions
| Product / approach | What it does | Strengths | Weaknesses | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## What users need
- <need or pain point> ([source](url))

## Constraints and risks
- <constraint> ([source](url))

## Options for us
1. **<option>** - pros, cons, rough effort (S/M/L)

## Open questions
- <what we could not verify>

## Sources
- [title](url) - accessed <date>
```

## Quality bar
- Every factual claim links to a source. No invented numbers or quotes.
- Separate facts from your opinion; the recommendation is clearly labelled as yours.
- Under 800 words unless asked for more.
