# Writing content

## Brief
Pin down before writing: topic, reader (who, how expert), the one thing they should take away, the call to action, length (default 900-1400 words), and the primary search keyword.

## Research
- Read 3-5 good sources and note facts with links. Find the angle they miss: a concrete example, a step they skip, a common mistake.
- Use current information; date anything that may change.

## Structure
1. Title: specific and honest, includes the keyword naturally (under 65 characters).
2. Intro: the reader's problem in two or three sentences, and what they will get.
3. Body: H2 sections that each answer one question; examples, steps, code or screenshots where they help.
4. Conclusion: the takeaway and one call to action.

## File template

```markdown
---
title: "<title>"
description: "<meta description, under 155 characters>"
date: <YYYY-MM-DD>
tags: [<tag>, <tag>]
draft: true
---

<post>

## Sources
- [title](url)
```

## Style
- Write like you talk to one smart reader: second person, active voice, short sentences.
- Show, don't claim: examples beat adjectives.
- No filler intros ("In today's fast-paced world"), no keyword stuffing, no invented statistics.
- Original wording; short quotes only, always linked.
