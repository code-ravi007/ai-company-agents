# Launch kit

## Before writing
- Who is it for (one specific audience), what problem does it solve, and what is different from the alternatives?
- Collect the proof that exists: what the feature really does, screenshots or docs in the repository, real numbers only if they are in the repository.

## Positioning statement
For <audience> who <need>, <product> is a <category> that <key benefit>. Unlike <alternative>, it <difference>.

## Template (one file)

```markdown
# Launch kit: <feature or product>

## Positioning
<the statement above>

## Key messages
1. <benefit, in the user's words> - proof: <what in the product shows this>
2. ...
3. ...

## Landing page copy
**Headline:** <8 words or fewer, benefit first>
**Subheadline:** <one sentence: what it is and for whom>
**Feature blocks:** 3 x (title + 1-2 sentences)
**Call to action:** <button text>

## Social posts
- **X / Twitter** (under 280 characters): ...
- **LinkedIn** (3-5 short paragraphs): ...
- **Reddit / Hacker News** (plain, no hype, what and why, invite feedback): ...

## Launch email
Subject: <under 50 characters>  
Preview text: <under 90 characters>  
Body: <under 150 words, one call to action>

## SEO
- Primary keyword: ...
- Secondary keywords: ...
- Meta title (under 60 characters): ...
- Meta description (under 155 characters): ...

## Launch checklist
- [ ] <where to post, in order>
```

## Rules
- Benefits before features; plain words, no buzzwords ("revolutionary", "seamless", "unlock").
- Only true claims. No invented testimonials, user counts, ratings or press.
- Respect each platform's norms; Reddit and Hacker News punish hype.
